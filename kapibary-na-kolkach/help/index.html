Help: Capybaras on Wheels: Racing

Here are answers to the most common questions about the game. If something is missing, write to us: pondoodlegames@yahoo.com.

What is free and what the purchase adds

The download is free, and this part stays free for good:

  • the Prologue and all of Trip 1 with five racers,
  • Time Trial,
  • the Daily Race,
  • Pass the Phone for 2 to 4 players on one phone.

The game has a single purchase: "Full game". You pay once, and it adds:

  • Trips 2 to 8 and the Final,
  • more racers,
  • Hard difficulty on the new trips,
  • twist trips: four tracks you know, with one rule changed.

There are no ads, no subscription and no currency sold for money. Pips can't be bought; you collect them on the track. The game plays offline. The store shows the current price.

I bought the full game but I can't see it

  • Wait a moment. While the store is still confirming the payment, the full game will open by itself.
  • Check that the phone is online and that the store is signed in to the same account that made the purchase. Restoring a purchase needs a connection to the store.
  • In the game, open Settings, find the For grown-ups section and tap Restore purchase. A short question for an adult comes first. The same button is on the "Full game" card.
  • Trips open one after another: finishing a trip opens the next one. If Trip 1 isn't finished yet, finish it first.

If that didn't help, write to us and include the order number from the store receipt. Don't send card details.

Family and a second device

iPhone and iPad. The purchase works with Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group can use it. On the second device, install the game and tap Restore purchase. Family Sharing and purchase sharing are set up in Apple's settings.

Android. The purchase belongs to the Google account you buy it with. On another phone, sign in to Google Play with the same account and tap Restore purchase.

Refunds

The store handles the payment, not us, so refunds go through the store too. For the App Store, make a request at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, the rules and the form are in Google Play Help. The store makes the decision.

Controls and assists

You choose how to steer on first launch, and you can change it at any time in Settings, under Steering.

  • Touch strip. Steer with your finger on the strip on the left side of the screen.
  • Tilt. Steer by tilting the phone left and right. Calibrate sets the position in which the vehicle drives straight, and Sensitivity sets how strongly it reacts.
  • Drift. Steer and hold Drift, then let go on the straight. Held without steering, it slows you down.

Assists are in Settings, under Assists:

  • One thumb. The game steers along the line for you, and you use the items. Good for a first try and for playing with one hand.
  • Helping wheel. The game gently lines your vehicle up with the road. It works in Time Trial too, and the run is then marked as assisted.
  • Auto-drift. The game drifts into corners for you, and you just steer. It is off while One thumb is on.

Settings also has a Layout editor (move and resize the buttons), a Left-handed layout and Reduce camera motion.

The save and a new phone

The game has no accounts and no cloud of its own. The save (your progress, pips and settings) is kept only on the device. Uninstalling the game removes it from the device.

If system backup is turned on (Google on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone), the system may keep a copy of the save and restore it on a new phone. The system and your account manage that backup. We have no access to it, so we can't bring back a lost save.

Leaderboards and achievements in Google Play Games and Game Center don't carry your progress. The purchase doesn't depend on the save: on a new phone, just tap Restore purchase.

Privacy

The game has no accounts, ads or analytics, and its code sends nothing over the internet. Data leaves the device only through Google and Apple services: the store purchase, the optional leaderboards, the rating window, and when you choose to share something yourself. The details are in the game's privacy policy.
